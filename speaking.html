How it works

Every answer comes back with a transcript and analysis

The AI examiner asks a question, you speak into the mic, and your answer is transcribed and scored on Fluency, Lexical Resource, Grammar, and Pronunciation.

Try a sample

PART 3 · "Do you think technology has changed education?"

"Um, yes, I think, technology is very good thing for, um, education because students can access so many resource online."

Fluency: frequent "um" fillers and hesitations — this lowers your fluency score.
Lexical Resource: "very good thing" is vague — try a more precise word like "highly beneficial" or "transformative."

Why most learners score lower on Speaking

01

Memorizing a rehearsed answer

Examiners spot templated answers instantly — it hurts your Fluency score.

02

Short, one-word answers

"Yes, I like it" wastes the chance to develop an idea — add a reason and an example.

03

Fear of unfamiliar topics

Part 3 tests logical reasoning, not deep expert knowledge.

Speak for 5 minutes today

Small, consistent practice sessions significantly reduce exam-day tension.
