Know exactly where each score comes from

  • Task Achievement — whether you fully address the prompt with a clear position.
  • Coherence & Cohesion — whether your ideas flow logically and linking is used well.
  • Lexical Resource — the range and precision of your vocabulary.
  • Grammatical Range & Accuracy — variety of sentence structures and grammatical correctness.

In conclusion, I think that governments should invest more in public transport because it reduce traffic and pollution in big cities.

Coherence: "I think that" is too personal and weak for a conclusion — try a more formal alternative like "Overall, it can be argued that."
Grammar: "it reduce" should be "it reduces" to agree with the third-person singular subject.
