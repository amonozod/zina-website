The most common mistakes

01

Ignoring the word limit

Instructions like "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS" are automatically marked wrong if broken — even when the meaning is correct.

02

Missing the paraphrase

The audio and the question rarely use the same words — "accommodation" might appear in the audio as "place to stay."

03

Spelling errors

Hearing the right word but writing it incorrectly — especially with place names and numbers.

04

Getting stuck on one question

Dwelling on a missed answer often costs you the next 2–3 questions too — timed practice fixes this.
